# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. Because *Salalihini Sandeshaya* is a cherished work of Sri Lankan cultural heritage, we ask all participants to approach discussions of its language, history, and religious traditions (Buddhist and Hindu alike) with warmth, humility, and mutual respect.

## Our Standards

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and interpretations
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism on translations and code
